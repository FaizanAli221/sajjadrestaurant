A table on the Arabian Sea

Sajjad Restaurant sits on Beach Avenue at Do Darya, DHA Phase 8 — the stretch of Karachi coastline that only truly wakes up after sunset. Every seat looks out at the water, and the kitchen works in view of the floor.

We cook Pakistani, barbecue, Chinese and continental dishes from the best ingredients we can source that morning. The guest is the focus of everything we do: fresh food, generous portions, attentive service, and a room that works as well for two people as it does for a family of twenty.

  • Live grill and open kitchen
  • Family seating, kids' play area and valet parking
  • Event and large-group booking
  • Cards accepted · free Wi-Fi
